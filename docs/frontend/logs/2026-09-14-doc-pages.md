## Decisions worth keeping

- Stale prose is named, not served. The same conditional rule that refuses
  to *write* prose over moved members refuses to *show* it.
- Backlinks are a query, not a column (the 2026-09-06 note's reason holds:
  a stored reverse index must be swept on every write).
- `docpage` is registered as a loud failure rather than left out of the
  handler map, so a job of that kind can never sit queued and silent.
