# Activity list, badge contrast and reduced motion — 2026-09-25

Status: **built and tested locally, e2e included; production after merge** (salvaged from the unmerged Data Brain sweeps, DBR-3.4; each defect re-checked on main first).

Three accessibility defects that the suite's axe run could not see, because it checks colour contrast on resting surfaces only.

| Surface                                                                                           | Defect on main                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Change                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `/map` and `/app/map` activity rows (`ui/dashboard-screen.tsx`, `app/(shell)/map/map-screen.tsx`) | The rows sat in `role="feed"`, which may only own articles; they are buttons. axe `aria-required-children`, **critical** (reproduced on main in a browser).                                                                | A named `ul`, one `li` per row: a list of calls. `map-hud.css` resets the list and its selectors now read `> li > button`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Status badges (`styles/primitives.css`, `styles/tokens.css`)                                      | Badges painted their grade's accent. On the hovered and selected row grounds that measured 4.33:1 / 4.28:1 (light `inferred`), 4.46:1 (light `verified` on selected) and 4.17:1 (dark `broken` on selected), under AA 4.5. | Badges paint small-text tones: canonical `--fg-success` and `--fg-danger` (new, beside `--attention-text`) and `--attention-text`. `--fg-success` is the accent in dark and `#116329` in light (6.50:1); `--fg-danger` is `#ff7b72` in dark (5.55:1) and the accent in light. The legacy `--verified-text` / `--danger-text` aliases now point at them. |
| Canvas camera (`ui/brain-map.tsx`, `ui/brain-map-stage.tsx`, new `lib/motion/reduced-motion.ts`)  | The stylesheets honour `prefers-reduced-motion`; the camera glided to every focused node regardless (WCAG 2.3.3).                                                                                                          | Under the setting the camera's time constant is 0, so a move is a jump; the stage reports `data-motion="reduced" \| "full"` and follows the setting while the page is open.                                                                                                                                                                             |

Tests:

- `tests/design-tokens.test.ts`: status badges clear 4.5:1 on every surface plus `--bg-hover` and `--bg-selected`, in both themes. The colour is read from the stylesheet. On main it names the three failing pairs above plus light `inferred` on `--code-bg` (4.33:1).
- `apps/web/lib/motion/reduced-motion.test.ts`: under reduced motion the camera arrives in one frame; otherwise it still glides.
- `tests/e2e/activity-list-a11y.spec.ts`: after a replay, the activity list holds five buttons in five list items and axe finds no `aria-required-children`, `aria-required-parent`, `list` or `listitem` violation. The stage follows `emulateMedia({ reducedMotion })` both ways. On main's markup both tests fail (no list, no `data-motion`), and axe reports `aria-required-children` (critical) on the feed.
- `tests/e2e/live-graph.spec.ts` now finds the rows by role `list` and name.
- Local e2e against the local Supabase stack: 13 specs, 114 passed; after the badges moved to the canonical `--fg-*` names (same colours), the contrast, theme, activity, live-graph, dashboard and brain-map specs again, 94 passed. Every dark/light sweep in `a11y-contrast.spec.ts` and `screens-theme.spec.ts` passed. The only failures, three each run, were `/app/connect/github`, which needs `GITHUB_APP_ID`; this worktree's env file carries local Supabase values only.

Not changed: the pane collapse controls (OQ-071 on the Data Brain branch) and the Data Brain explorer itself. The DBR `<dl>` violation lived only in that branch's new component.
